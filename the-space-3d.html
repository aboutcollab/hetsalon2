The six plots are open and demarcated, not closed rooms — 1.2 m screens between plots, and a curtain where Studios 1 and 2 meet the work floor (shown gathered open). Nothing is lockable. That is the honest picture of what is being offered. The saw room is the exception: walls up to the deck and one door, because the noise has to stay inside it.

Stair layout is still being designed: drawn here as compliant straight runs. The structure — 43 timber posts, beams, 22 mm deck — is Peter’s V2 plan.
